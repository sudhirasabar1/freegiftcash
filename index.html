GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Availability and reward eligibility depend on each third-party provider’s terms and conditions.

Why might an offer ask for a survey or signup?

Some partner offers have specific participation requirements. Review them carefully before deciding whether to continue.

How can I stay safe?

Use official websites, read privacy policies, avoid suspicious links, and only share information when you understand why it is needed.
